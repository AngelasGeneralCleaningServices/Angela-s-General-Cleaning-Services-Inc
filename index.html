About Our Company

Dedicated To Creating Healthier & Brighter Living Spaces

Welcome to Angela's General Cleaning Services Inc, your premier residential and commercial cleaning provider based in Woodbridge, VA. We take pride in transforming busy houses, offices, and post-construction sites into immaculate, refreshed environments.

Our skilled team utilizes high-efficiency tools, eco-conscious cleaning techniques, and standard quality checks to guarantee that every corner receives detailed attention. Whether you require weekly maintenance, an intensive move-out cleanup, or exterior pressure washing, we are committed to exceptional standards.

Our Services

House Cleaning

Complete routine housekeeping tailored to your daily life. Dusting, vacuuming, kitchen sanitation, bathroom scrubbing, and bed making for a neat, welcoming home.

Deep Cleaning & Spring Cleaning

An intensive top-to-bottom scrub down tackling grime, baseboards, behind appliances, inside cabinets, and hard-to-reach spots to give your property a total reset.

Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning

Ensure your full security deposit refund or welcome home to a pristine blank slate. Thorough turnover cleaning services for landlords, renters, and home buyers.

Post-Construction Cleaning

Removal of heavy drywall dust, paint speckles, debris, and residue post-remodel. We turn messy construction sites into move-in ready spaces.

Carpet Cleaning

Deep fiber extraction to remove stubborn stains, pet dander, trapped allergens, and odor. Revitalize your rugs and wall-to-wall carpets effortlessly.

Power Washing

High-pressure washing for exterior sidings, driveways, decks, walkways, and patios. Effectively blasts away mildew, algae, grime, and oil stains.

Gutter Cleaning

Clearing clogged leaves, sludge, and debris from gutters and downspouts to prevent roof leaks, foundation water damage, and pest infestations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply your own cleaning products and equipment?

Yes! Angela's General Cleaning Services Inc brings all professional-grade, eco-friendly cleaning supplies, vacuums, pressure washers, and microfiber materials needed to complete the job.

How are your estimates calculated?

Estimates are based on property size (square footage), overall condition, type of service (standard house cleaning vs deep/move-out/post-construction), and any special add-ons requested.

What areas do you serve around Woodbridge, VA?

We proudly serve Woodbridge, VA (22193) as well as nearby communities including Dale City, Lake Ridge, Dumfries, Manassas, Lorton, and Prince William County.

Do I need to be home while you clean?

It is entirely up to you! Many of our regular clients provide a door key or entry lockcode. Our vetted cleaning technicians are fully trustworthy and respectful of your home.
